FAQ

Does it work without a GPU?

Yes — the lite pose model runs comfortably on CPU. ~30 fps on most modern laptops.

Does it record me?

No. Frames are read from the webcam, fed straight into the pose detector, and discarded. Nothing is saved or uploaded. The only persisted data is your config (sliders, mute state) and a tiny per-day stats file with two integers.

Can I close the preview window and just keep the tray icon?

Yes. Right-click the tray icon and uncheck "Show preview". The detector keeps running in the background; you'll only see toasts when posture goes bad.

What if it keeps false-alarming?

Open the preview window and slide the relevant axis up — e.g., if it nags about head tilt while you're reading, raise the "Head tilt deg" slider. Settings are saved automatically.

Can I move my chair without recalibrating?

Small drift is fine — the metrics are normalized by shoulder width, so distance to the camera doesn't matter much. Big changes (new desk, different camera) → recalibrate from the tray menu.
